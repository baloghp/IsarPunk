## The story we tell (Save Oma — cross-generational)

**Problem:** Millions of PCs in Germany still run Windows 10. After consumer security updates end (**Oct 2027**), unpatched machines — especially those used by **Oma and Opa** — are magnets for scams, ransomware, and phishing. Many seniors don’t notice the “software cliff.” At the same time, still-usable hardware gets thrown away.

**Our angle:** We teach **young people (~12–15)** in Munich schools about reuse, FOSS, and safer alternatives — not as abstract IT, but as something they can **bring home**. The hook is **Save Oma**: *your grandparent’s PC can stay fast and secure; you can be the one who helps.*

**Your role in the story:** We spark confidence and awareness in the classroom. **You** finish the job — repair, revive, Linux install, or honest recycle. Kids and families arrive with context, not cold confusion.

```text
School workshop (kids ~12–15)
    → “Talk to Oma / Opa” + take-home sheet
        → Family brings device to your shop / café
            → Cross-generational win: secure PC, less e-waste, proud grandchild
```

## Elevator (≈60 seconds)

> We’re **IsarPunk** — *Hack your life / Save Oma* — a Munich project funded via Nebenan Stiftung.  
> We run workshops for **kids around 12 to 15** about the Windows 10 cliff, circular reuse, and Linux / open source.  
> The mission is **cross-generational**: students learn enough to help **Oma and Opa** — safer, faster PCs instead of trashing good hardware.  
> We **don’t** repair machines ourselves. We teach, then **send families to partners like you**.  
> Partners get workshop referrals **and** visibility on Circular Munich’s [CircularCity Map](https://circular-munich.com/circularcitymap/).  
> Can we take 10 minutes to see if a light partnership makes sense?

## What we offer you

### 1. Customer traffic (main value) — often cross-generational
- Workshop kids leave with: *“Here’s where our family can take Oma’s laptop.”*  
- You get **family pairs or trios** (teen + parent + grandparent device), not only solo teens.  
- You appear on our **partner list / take-home sheet**.  
- Soft ask later: “Did anyone mention IsarPunk / Save Oma?” — so we can prove impact together.

### 2. Visibility
- Listing in IsarPunk handouts and partner sheet.  
- Listing on Circular Munich’s **[CircularCity Map](https://circular-munich.com/circularcitymap/)** — public map of circular B2C touchpoints in the city (we help get you on it as part of partnership).  
- Optional: **Climate Week** / other Circular Munich visibility (when relevant).  
- Story angle for you: *the shop where kids send their grandparents.*

### 3. Qualified, not random, demand
- People arrive with a story: *“Win10 is ending / Oma’s PC is slow / we want Linux or a second life”* — not “fix my toaster for free.”  
- We **filter** by your strength: hardware repair vs software/Linux-only cafés.

### 4. Social proof / community
- Photos / short interview only **with your OK** (social media team may join a visit day).  
- You’re part of a **cross-generational circular electronics** story schools and families actually care about.

## What we ask from you

Keep it light. Ideal partner says yes to **at least (A)**; (B)–(D) are bonuses.

| | Ask | Notes |
|---|-----|------|
| **A** | May we **list you** as a place families can go after our workshops — on IsarPunk materials **and** the [CircularCity Map](https://circular-munich.com/circularcitymap/)? | Name, address, website/phone, open hours if simple |
| **B** | Will you take **repair / Linux-install** customers we send — including **seniors’ devices**? | Even “software help only” is valuable |
| **C** | If a device is **completely dead**, can you still take it (recycle / parts / advise)? | Honest no is fine — we’ll route elsewhere |
| **D** | Optional: once in a while, **confirm** that someone came via IsarPunk (tick / WhatsApp / later app) | Helps our funding report; 10 seconds |
| **E** | Optional: **patient with seniors** or family bring-along? | Say if yes — we’ll highlight it on the handout |

**Not required:** hosting our workshops, free repairs, sponsorship money, evenings on call.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| “We’re too busy.” | Totally fair. Listing only — no obligation to discount or prioritise. |
| “Kids will waste our time.” | Workshops target **12–15**; we tell them you’re a **real shop / skilled volunteers** — bring the device, normal process. Often it’s **Oma’s laptop**, with a parent or teen. |
| “Seniors are difficult customers.” | That’s exactly why we prep kids first — families arrive with a story and a cheat sheet, not a blind panic call. |
| “We don’t do Linux.” | Great — say so. We’ll send Win10/hardware cases, or only cafés that do FOSS. |
| “We don’t take e-waste.” | OK — we’ll only send repairable cases and point dead devices elsewhere. |
| “What’s in it for us?” | Warm **family** leads + IsarPunk listing + **CircularCity Map** presence + Save Oma story; no fee from us. |
| “Can you pay for repairs?” | No — grant isn’t for that. Families pay your normal rates (or café donation model). |
